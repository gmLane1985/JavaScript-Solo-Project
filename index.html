JavaScript Solo Project - Garrett Lane

In this solo project we were tasked with creating three 'programs'.

  • A rock paper scissors game that:
    1. randomly generates computer moves
    2. determines the winner
    3. outputs the result
  • A pig latin program that:
    1. converts each word according to Pig Latin rules
    2. outputs the translated text
  • A Caesar decipher program that:
    1. takes a phrase and a shift number
    2. applies the shift (both positive and negative)
    3. outputs the encrypted text

Let's play Rock, Paper, Scissors, Lizard, Spock!

Rules to the game

You will be playing against the computer.

You can choose between Rock, Paper, Scissors, Lizard, and Spock.

The first one to three points wins.

Here are the rules to getting a point in the game:

  • Rock crushes Scissors
  • Rock crushes Lizard
  • Paper covers Rock
  • Paper disproves Spock
  • Scissors cuts Paper
  • Scissors decapitates Lizard
  • Lizard poisons Spock
  • Lizard eats Paper
  • Spock breaks Scissors
  • Spock vaporizes Rock

If the player and computer choose the same option (Ex. Paper and Paper), then no one gets the point.
